03

Where I fell for data

In ice dance, a panel of nine judges grades every element, and placements can come down to hundredths of a point. After each competition I studied the score sheets (called protocols) to find exactly where we were gaining and losing points, then turned that into specific training goals.

How a protocol worksExample
ElementBaseJ1J2J3J4J5J6J7J8J9Score
Twizzles6.5+2+2+3+1+2+2+1+207.6
Lift5.3+1+2+1+3+1+20+1+26.1
Steps8.00+1-10+1+20+108.3
Spin4.5+2+2+1+3+2+1+2+2+25.3

Illustrative numbers, not a real protocol. Each judge grades the execution of an element; the highest and lowest grades are dropped, and the rest are averaged and converted into points added to the element's base value. The highlighted row is the kind of weak spot I looked for.

Study→Analyze→Improve

Study the protocol after every event, element by element and judge by judge.

Analyze where the points were leaking: a level we didn't reach, or an element the judges consistently marked down.

Improve by turning that into training goals for the next competition. In our final season, that process helped us add 10 points to our total score.
