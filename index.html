Airline Repairables Analytics · May 2026

Finding the noise hiding in $29B of parts spend

PythonpandasPlotlyLLM-assisted coding

A major U.S. airline's repair forecasts were built on spend data that didn't reflect real demand. Our team analyzed 3.43 million repairable parts transactions to find out why.

  • Built a monthly noise scorecard and an interactive Plotly dashboard
  • Isolated 4 distortion sources: unused kit returns, multi-station "chasing the plane," scrap and orphaned transactions
  • Quantified $22.8B in chaser activity and recommended financial controls and parts traceability for a clean forecasting baseline
