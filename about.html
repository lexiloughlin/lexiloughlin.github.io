01On the ice

Skating was my whole world from the time I was a kid. As a teenager I moved abroad to compete for Azerbaijan's senior national ice dance team, training in Minsk, earning Azerbaijani citizenship and learning Russian along the way. I lived on my own, trained more than 40 hours a week, kept up AP and honors classes remotely, and handled every visa and flight for my partner and me.

What I loved was the craft: the music, the precision, hitting the beat exactly right.

Nine judges score every element in ice dance, and placements come down to hundredths of a point. So I started reading the scoring sheets like a puzzle, looking for exactly where we were losing points. In our final season we raised our total by 10 points, and became Azerbaijan's top senior ice dance team. We competed in the U.S., Canada, Germany, Poland, Belarus, Hungary and Croatia, and somewhere in all those score sheets I found out I love data.

02On stage

Before and after the ice, there was dance. I trained in ballet and a handful of other styles, and I love singing and performing. Stage over screen, always. There's nothing like the feeling of something you've practiced a hundred times landing exactly right in front of an audience.

03At the easel

Painting is where I slow down. It gives me something finished I can actually hold, and it trains the same eye I use at work: noticing the small detail everyone else walks past. Some of my best ideas also come from solo adventures, like dinner for one at a new restaurant, a museum or a show.

04In the work

I don't want to watch the future get built from the sidelines. I want to be in the room building it. I'm drawn to energy, innovation and the big systems that decide how the world runs, and I like to understand how something really works before I form an opinion about it.

Give me a hard problem, a team that shows up, and a reason it matters.

I'm at my best around ambitious people, working through a problem out loud until the pieces click. I also co-founded The Blessed Pear, a faith-based jewelry brand, and I keep a running list of rabbit holes: sustainable finance, AI, and the occasional true crime deep dive.

05With people

Very few people have taken my path, from score sheets in Minsk to data in Dallas, and it shapes how I see things. I look at problems from angles other people miss, because I've had to figure things out in very different worlds.

I want to learn from everyone I meet.

I love connecting with people from all over the world and soaking up as much as I can from them. Years of coaching taught me to take feedback and use it the very next day, so I'm extremely coachable and I learn fast.

06At home

Home is four guinea pigs: Toki, Mochi, Poke and Miso Soup. They star in every game I build, alongside Masaki, an investment banker pig of my own invention who wears a pinstripe suit and runs his own bank in Pig City.
