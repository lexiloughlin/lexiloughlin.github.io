Skating

I started skating as a kid in New Jersey, and it became my whole world. As a teenager I moved abroad to compete for Azerbaijan's senior national ice dance team, training in Minsk and earning Azerbaijani citizenship so I could compete internationally.

I lived on my own, learned Russian, trained more than 40 hours a week and kept up AP and honors classes remotely. I also handled the visas and travel for my partner and me across every competition.

What I loved most was the craft: the music, the precision, and hitting a beat exactly right. Ice dance is judged by nine judges on every element, so I studied the scoring data to find where we were losing points. In our final season we raised our total score by 10 points, became Azerbaijan's #1 senior team and qualified for the World Championships.
